Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to know what course I want before contacting Vikanns?

No. We can help you explore suitable academic options based on your qualifications, interests, career goals and budget.

Can Vikanns help with visa applications?

We provide guidance and application support, including document preparation and understanding relevant requirements. Visa decisions are made by the appropriate government authority, and we cannot guarantee approval.

Do you help with study loans?

Yes. Where available and where you meet the relevant eligibility requirements, we help students explore study funding and loan options.

Can Vikanns help with proof of funds?

Yes. We provide guidance around proof-of-funds requirements and available solutions for eligible applicants.

Which countries do you work with?

Our available destinations and programmes change according to current opportunities and institutional requirements. Contact us for the destinations currently available for your profile.

Can I contact Vikanns if I'm not sure where I want to study?

Absolutely. That's exactly what our consultation process is designed for.
